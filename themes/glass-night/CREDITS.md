# glass-night

A day and a night wallpaper behind a frosted glass dock and glass pills. `wallpaper.jpg` shows by day,
`wallpaper_night.jpg` from `night_from` to `night_until` (18:00 to 06:00 here), with a crossfade.

- `wallpaper.jpg`: Área de Proteção Ambiental da Baleia Franca, by Claudio Seelig (Ctseelig), CC BY-SA 4.0,

- `wallpaper_night.jpg`: 039 Northern lights over Mývatn (Iceland), by Giles Laurent, CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:039_Northern_lights_over_M%C3%BDvatn_(Iceland)_Photo_by_Giles_Laurent.jpg

Both centre-cropped to 1920x1080 and recompressed.
